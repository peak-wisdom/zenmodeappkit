Backgrounds, privacy, and printing

The built-in backgrounds are original occasion-matched vector illustrations. A design code controls the composition, palette, and decorative details, so you can keep creating variations and return to a favorite. Changing the occasion keeps custom wording; use Try suggested words when you want a fresh message.

Your draft saves in this browser, including its custom image when browser storage is available. Download an editable card as a backup or to continue on another device. Private browsing, storage limits, or clearing site data can remove browser saves. Image links load only when you press Load image link. Imported pictures are embedded, so opening a saved card does not contact the original image host.

PNG and the two-sided PDF use 300 pixels per printed inch. SVG artwork scales cleanly; uploaded photos retain their own image detail. Card sizes are exact in the SVG and PDF. The preview’s shadow and page background are not exported. Fonts may vary between devices in an editable SVG; PNG and PDF preserve this device’s appearance. Use photos you have permission to use.
