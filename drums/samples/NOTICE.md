Bundled files: `kick-01.wav`, `snare-01.wav`, `hihat-closed.wav`, `hihat-open.wav`, `tom-01.wav`, `tom-03.wav`, `ride-01.wav`, and `crash-01.wav`. The bundled WAV files are unmodified. At playback/export, the app normalizes, adjusts levels, and arranges the recordings into beats; hats may be shortened by the closed-hat choke. Downloaded projects include these credits, and exported WAVs carry them in a RIFF INFO comment.
