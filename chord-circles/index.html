A little guide to the circle

Moving clockwise in fifths takes C to G to D and onward through all twelve pitches. Change the direction to explore fourths instead. Each inner chord is the relative minor of the outer major chord above it.

Highlighted chords use only notes from your chosen scale. The seven buttons show chords built by stacking thirds in that scale, including diminished and augmented chords that may not appear as a major/minor wheel button. Harmonic and melodic minor use their displayed forms throughout.

Adjacent keys often share notes. A dominant seventh resolving down a fifth can sound like arriving home. Try G7 followed by C, or the full circle of dominants. All pitches use twelve-tone equal temperament, with A4 at 440 Hz.
